## 🙏 About This Book

This book provides a comprehensive and accessible introduction to the major themes, concepts, and schools of Buddhist philosophy. It is written for the general reader — no prior knowledge of Buddhism is assumed — while also seeking to convey the depth and sophistication of the tradition.

### Key Features

- **15 detailed chapters** covering the full breadth of Buddhist philosophy
- **Historical context** tracing Buddhism from its origins to the present day
- **Clear explanations** of core concepts with original Pali and Sanskrit terminology
- **Practical applications** including meditation instructions and ethical guidelines
- **Cross-traditional perspective** covering Theravada, Mahayana, and Vajrayana traditions
- **Modern relevance** including Buddhism's dialogue with science and contemporary issues

### How to Read This Book

Each chapter builds upon the previous ones, creating a cumulative understanding of Buddhist philosophy. Readers new to Buddhism are encouraged to read the chapters in order. Those with some background may choose to focus on specific topics of interest.
