# Buddhism-philosophy

## Philosophical Syncretism: A Study of Dharma and Karuna as Catalysts for Social Cohesion

**MPhil Thesis Proposal — Eastern Philosophy**

### Overview

This repository contains the thesis proposal and supporting research materials for an MPhil thesis in Eastern Philosophy investigating how the philosophical concepts of **Dharma** (righteousness, moral law) and **Karuṇā** (compassion) function syncretically across Hindu and Buddhist traditions as catalysts for **social cohesion** in **Jhapa District, Nepal**.

### Contents

- **[THESIS_PROPOSAL.md](THESIS_PROPOSAL.md)** — Complete thesis proposal including:
  - Theoretical and Empirical Literature Review
  - Research Gap Identification
  - Statement of Problems
  - Research Questions and Objectives
  - Research Methodology (Exploratory Sequential Mixed Methods: QUAL → QUAN → Integration)
  - 77 Genuine Academic References

### Key Concepts

| Concept | Hindu Tradition | Buddhist Tradition |
|---|---|---|
| **Dharma** | Ṛta, svadharma, sāmānya dharma | Dhamma, Noble Eightfold Path, pratītyasamutpāda |
| **Karuṇā** | Dayā, ahiṃsā, divine quality | Brahmavihāra, mahākaruṇā, Bodhisattva ideal |

### Study Area

Jhapa District, Province No. 1, Eastern Terai, Nepal — a religiously and ethnically diverse region with significant Hindu-Buddhist co-existence.

### Philosophical Limitation

The study is limited to **Hinduism** and **Buddhism** as the two major philosophical traditions under investigation.
